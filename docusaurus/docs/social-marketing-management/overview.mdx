## Service Guide: Social Media Management

The Social Media Management service team creates and publishes strategic content for your social media accounts. This service helps maintain a consistent online presence, build brand loyalty, and engage with your audience through data-driven content.

This service includes:
* Strategic content creation and publishing.
* Custom image creation.
* Monthly content calendars for review and approval.
* SEO-enhanced blogs.
* Facebook Post Boosting.

---

## What is the Social Media Management Service?

This service handles the day-to-day tasks of creating and scheduling posts for your business's social media profiles. The service uses a combination of AI-assisted tools and expert oversight to create content. Captions and hashtags are generated by AI and then refined by specialists for tone and platform goals. Blog titles and outlines are also AI-assisted before being fully written and edited by the team. This process ensures your business is represented by a social media presence that is engaging and professional.

---

## Why is this Service Important?

A strong social media strategy is crucial for connecting with your target audience, building trust, and increasing visibility. This service solves the challenge of maintaining an active presence by providing a dedicated team to handle content creation, publishing, and strategy. This allows you to benefit from targeted content, audience engagement, and data-driven decisions that ultimately drive business growth.

## What’s Included

The service is available in two tiers with different deliverables. Posts can be shared across any supported platform with no extra charge. Supported platforms are Facebook, X, Instagram, LinkedIn, Google Business Profile, and Pinterest.

### Standard Service
* **12 Unique Posts and Stories per Month:** The posting schedule is tailored to your content needs and may vary week to week, such as posting more during a campaign or event.
* **4 Custom Images per Month:** Unique images are created to align with your brand for specific content like promotions or new product launches.
* **2 Story Highlights (Reposts) per Month:** High-engagement content is strategically reposted as a story highlight.
* **$25 USD in Facebook Post Boosting:** One post per month is chosen by the team to boost, though you can request a specific post be chosen.
* **1 Quarterly SEO Blog:** Includes one blog of up to 1500-2000 words per quarter, with social posts designed to promote it and drive traffic.

### Plus Service
This service includes all Standard features plus additional deliverables for a more robust strategy.
* **8 Additional Unique Posts and Stories per Month** (20 total).
* **6 Additional Custom Images per Month** (10 total).
* **3 Additional Story Highlights per Month** (5 total).
* **An additional $25 USD for Post Boosting** ($50 USD total).
* **2 Additional SEO-Enhanced Blogs per Quarter** (3 total).
* **Quarterly Strategy Calls:** These sessions review key metrics, post performance, and gather feedback. An updated strategy deck is presented, which includes keyword research, content pillars, and social profile recommendations.
* **Optional Monthly Check-Ins:** You can book optional monthly meetings for feedback or to address urgent updates.

## The Content Delivery & Review Process

The content creation and review process follows a clear monthly cycle.

1.  **First Content Calendar:** Within 5 business days of your onboarding call, you will receive your first month's social calendar and blog (if applicable) via email.
2.  **Ongoing Calendar Delivery:** For subsequent months, you can expect the content calendar to be delivered via email by the 25th of the current month for the following month's posts.
3.  **Content Submission Deadline:** Any new content ideas or requests for the next month's calendar must be shared with the team before the 10th of the current month. If no new requests are received, the team will create content based on the established strategy.
4.  **Review & Revisions:** You are asked to provide all revision requests in a single, consolidated round to ensure the calendar is delivered on time.
5.  **Automatic Publishing:** Content is automatically published 5 business days after the calendar has been sent to you for review. This is to ensure your service continues without interruption. Be sure to submit edit requests in a timely manner.

## Managing Your Content

You have full access to the scheduled content in the social media platform. You are welcome and encouraged to make quick tweaks or changes directly in the platform at any time.

## Troubleshooting

### Facebook Post Boosting
Boosting increases a post's visibility to a broader audience. However, some business types may not be eligible for boosting due to Meta's advertising guidelines. This includes, but is not limited to, businesses related to credit, employment, housing, insurance, and social or political issues. The service currently only offers boosting for Facebook posts.
